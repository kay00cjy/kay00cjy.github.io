Experience

I've had the opportunity to apply mechanical engineering in real product development settings through internships at two HATCH Ventures startups — working on rehabilitation devices and composite manufacturing tooling.

Internship 01

TruMotion Technologies HATCH Ventures
Mechanical Engineering Intern
May 2026 – Present

TruMotion is developing a motor-driven rehabilitation device for lower-limb therapy. As a Mechanical Engineering Intern, I contribute to the mechanical optimization of the device — from redesigning load-bearing components to refining the ergonomics of the patient interface, with an eye toward production-ready manufacturability.

  • Optimized mechanical performance of a motor-driven rehabilitation device by redesigning load-bearing components using FEA, improving alignment accuracy to reduce mechanical backlash
  • Improved ergonomic and biomechanical functionality by refining footplate geometry, integrating mechanical safety limits to ensure safe operation in both clinical and home-based rehabilitation environments
  • Enhanced manufacturability by redesigning prototype components with tolerance, machining, and fabrication constraints in mind, while improving structural durability
  • Working with cross-functional teams to support the transition from prototype to production-ready design
FEA SolidWorks Tolerance Analysis Machining Ergonomic Design Prototyping

Internship 02

Sentact Technologies HATCH Ventures
Mechanical Engineering Intern
Jul 2026 – Aug 2026

Sentact develops custom composite manufacturing solutions. My internship focused on creating a fully parametric CAD workflow for a carbon fibre application mold, enabling rapid design updates while maintaining structural and compliance requirements for composite layup.

  • Developing a fully parametric CAD workflow for a customizable carbon fiber application mold, enabling rapid design updates based on client-specific dimensional and mechanical compliance requirements
  • Refining internal structural geometry and support sensor integration for composite manufacturing
  • Working with composite manufacturing constraints to ensure structural integrity and layup quality
