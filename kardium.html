Kardium Inc.

Mechanical Test Engineering Intern · September 2026–Present · Burnaby, BC

At Kardium, I support medical-device development through mechanical testing, fixture design, and data analysis. The work has strengthened how I connect engineering requirements with controlled, repeatable tests and well-documented results.

Focus
Mechanical V&V and test method validation
Hands-on work
Designing, machining, and assembling test fixtures
Approach
Controlled testing and statistical analysis

From requirements to reliable tests

  • Execute mechanical verification and validation tests under approved standard operating procedures, test protocols, and Test Method Validation requirements to support product and design decisions.
  • Design, machine, and assemble custom fixtures and jigs to improve loading uniformity and sample alignment for specific test conditions.
  • Work with risk and design teams to scope project requirements and contribute to test method validation procedures.
  • Conduct a characterization study on a new design feature, using systematic evaluation to understand mechanical behaviour.
  • Analyze mechanical test data statistically to assess validity, variability, and significance, then document findings using Good Documentation Practices.

Testing has taught me to ask why behind each design decision: how a component is loaded, how a fixture affects the measurement, and what the results can tell us about the design.
