02 · Tooling & manufacturing processes

Sentact Technologies

Mechanical Engineering Intern · July–August 2026 · Vancouver, BC
UBC HATCH Ventures

At Sentact, I worked on tooling and manufacturing processes for an automated fibre placement (AFP) roller used in carbon-fibre layup. The challenge was to make dimensional customization more efficient while producing consistent polyurethane-overmoulded parts.

Design
Parametric AFP roller workflow
Build & test
3D-printed moulds, casting, and injection moulding
Result
25% reduction in part defects

Making customization repeatable

  • Developed a fully parametric workflow connecting a Python dashboard with SolidWorks macros and configurations, enabling rapid dimensional customization of the roller.
  • Designed and fabricated custom 3D-printed moulds for polyurethane overmoulding, reducing part defects by 25%.
  • Validated functional roller prototypes through polyurethane casting and injection moulding, refining process parameters to improve reproducibility and consistency.
  • Iterated on tooling geometry, process parameters, and usability to reduce operator load and make the workflow more scalable.

This experience connected CAD automation with the realities of fabrication: a flexible model only becomes useful when the tooling and process can consistently produce the intended part.
