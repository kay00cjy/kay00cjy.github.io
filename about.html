My Story

How I got here

I've always been a builder. As a kid, it was popsicle sticks and straws — seeing how much weight a bridge could hold before it gave way. That same curiosity carries into everything I do today, just with better materials and slightly higher stakes.

Volunteering at the Alberta Children's Hospital opened my eyes to the real-world impact of medical technology — not just on patients, but on families and the clinicians who care for them. That experience made my path feel obvious: I wanted to be on the side building those tools.
