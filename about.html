My Story

Why engineering?

I first found a love of creating through music. As a young pianist, I enjoyed taking fundamental building blocks and making something that felt my own. Performing for patients and families at Alberta Children’s Hospital showed me that what I created could also make a difference to someone else.

That experience helped lead me to Biomedical Engineering at UBC, where I’m completing a Bachelor of Applied Science with a mechanical specialization. I’m drawn to medical devices that interact directly with the human body, as I find it interesting how biology and anatomy can intersect with engineering principles.

My internships have taken me from FEA and rehabilitation-device design at TruMotion, to manufacturing tooling and automation at Sentact, to mechanical testing at Kardium. Each opportunity has added another perspective on how design work should be approached, coming from considerations of usability, to cost-effectiveness, and most importantly, fullfillment of design requirements.
