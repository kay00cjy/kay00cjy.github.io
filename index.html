About Me

Why engineering?

Before engineering, I wanted to be a pianist. Performing for patients and families at Alberta Children’s Hospital showed me how something I created could make a difference to someone else. Biomedical engineering gave that impulse a new direction: building technology that supports people’s health and independence.

I'm now a Mechanical Test Engineering Intern at Kardium, working on medical-device verification and validation. Outside work, I lead the mechanical sub-team at UBC Bionic Hand and am learning to play electric guitar.
