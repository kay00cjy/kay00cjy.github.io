About Me

Why engineering?

I chose Biomedical Engineering because I loved design and working with my hands design, but I also wanted to make a meaningful impact on the community around me. Growing up around care homes and volunteering showed me how technology and infrastructure can transform quality of life. 3 years in, I think I've found the intersection I was searching for.

Currently, I'm a Mechanical Engineer intern at TruMotion Technologies, designing on mechanical systems for manufacture in rehabilitation devices. Outside of work, I spend time building an upper-limb prosthetic with UBC Bionics and learning to how to play the electric guitar!
